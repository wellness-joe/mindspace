Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data private?

Yes. All your mood entries, journal notes, and insights are stored 100% locally on your device. We have zero servers and never upload your data anywhere.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → select MindSpace → Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open MindSpace → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open MindSpace → Settings → scroll to the bottom → tap "Delete Account & Data". This permanently removes all your data from the device.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Export Data. Your mood logs and journal entries will be exported as a JSON file that you can save or share.

What is MindSpace PRO?

MindSpace PRO unlocks AI-powered insights, advanced analytics, unlimited journaling, smart reminders, and data export. Available as Monthly ($3.99/mo), Annual ($19.99/yr with 3-day free trial), or Lifetime ($49.99 one-time).

Does MindSpace work offline?

Yes, MindSpace works 100% offline. No internet connection is required to log moods, journal, use breathing exercises, or view insights.
